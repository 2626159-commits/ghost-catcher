

**Ghost Catcher** is a fast-paced 2D Halloween arcade game where players have 30 seconds to catch as many moving ghosts as possible by clicking or tapping them under time limit of 30 seconds. The ghost constantly floats and bounces around a haunted nighttime scene filled with a glowing moon, stars, bats, tombstones, pumpkins, fog, and purple-blue atmospheric lighting giving Halloween vibes. You can get points and even multiplier by clicking on ghost and creating catching streaks while any wrong click can decrease you points by '1' and multiplier by zero so be careful else the ghost will spook you👻.
